DRUM acts as a vital bridge between scientific unions, standards bodies, metrologists, and software developers. We address the complexity of digital unit representation by coordinating community efforts and advocating for standardized solutions.

### What the Group Does

*   **The DRUM Ambassadors Program:** We collaborate with a network of Ambassadors nominated by International Scientific Unions. The ambassadors survey current practices and advocate for unit standards within their specific fields.
*   **Advocacy & Action:** In 2022, DRUM published a landmark comment in *Nature* titled **"Stop squandering data: make units of measurement machine-readable"**, highlighting the cost of inconsistent units in scientific data.
*   **Inventories & Toolkits:** We maintain the Digital Unit Representation Inventory to index existing systems, and support prototype tools like UMIS and the SI Reference Point.
*   **Global Events:** We host workshops, webinars, and sessions at international conferences like SciDataCon and International Data Week to build consensus.
