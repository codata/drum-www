To enable both human researchers and automated machine learning systems to seamlessly process physical quantities, DRUM indexes semantic registries and supports APIs that validate and align unit definitions.

*   **APIs & Data Services:** Access the Fundamental Physical Constants API and knowledge base designed for AI systems and data science workflows.
*   **Digital Standards & Toolkits:** Explore the SI Reference Point and key semantic unit ontologies including QUDT and UCUM.
*   **Publications & Guides:** Consult the CODATA-DRUM implementation guide and landmark publications on machine-actionable metrology.
