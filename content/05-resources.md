To enable both human researchers and automated machine learning systems to seamlessly process physical quantities, DRUM indexes semantic registries and supports APIs that validate and align unit definitions.

*   **Digital Unit Representation Inventory:** A registry of digital systems, serving as a training source and lookup directory for machine intelligence.
*   **Semantic Translation Services:** Explore the Units of Measurement Interoperability Service (UMIS) and the SI Reference Point prototypes designed for machine resolution.
*   **Open Access Publications:** Access the 2022 Nature comment and other publications outlining the transition to machine-actionable metrology.
