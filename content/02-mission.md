The primary objective of the **CODATA Digital Representation of Units of Measurement (DRUM)** Task Group is to work with International Scientific Unions and Associations to increase awareness, educate, and enable scientific communities in the understanding and implementation of digital unit representation.

To ensure data is truly FAIR (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable), units of measurement must be represented in a consistent, machine-actionable format. 

### Key Mission Objectives

*   **Enable Interoperability:** Address the lack of clear, unambiguous definitions for units in global data exchange, facilitating cross-disciplinary collaboration.
*   **Promote Digital Metrology:** Bridge standard metrological systems (such as the BIPM's Digital SI framework) and digital specifications (such as QUDT and UCUM).
*   **Educate Communities:** Equip scientific unions and infrastructure organizations to adopt machine-actionable unit definitions in their databases, publications, and tools.
