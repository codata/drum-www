Task Group Membership

DRUM brings together international experts in metrology, data standards, and AI/machine intelligence to make units of measurement machine-actionable and AI-ready.

Meeting Rhythm: every couple of weeks, rotating across regional time zones.

Get Involved

We welcome cooperation and participation from representatives of International Scientific Unions and Associations.
